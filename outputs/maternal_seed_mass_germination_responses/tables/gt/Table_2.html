Table 2. Source elevation, 100-seed mass and germination responses in 581 maternal accessions. H7a is time to first germination (T0), H7b mean germination rate (MGR), H7c germination percentage (GP), and H7d time spread (Tg-T0). H7d has no directional prediction. H8a, H8b and H8c concern T0, MGR and GP, respectively; H8 time spread also has no directional prediction. Coefficients and confidence limits are per 100-m source-elevation increase for H7 and per 1-mg increase in measured 100-seed mass for H8. Timing responses are in days, MGR in inverse days and GP in percentage points. P values test coefficients against zero. Each H8 response has an unadjusted and an elevation-adjusted model; Table S1 compares these pairs. Marginal_R2 and Conditional_R2 describe marginal and conditional R-squared. Convergence and singularity flags are retained from the fitted models.
