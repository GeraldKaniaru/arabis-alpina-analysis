Figure S6. Environmental variation across 27 source plots. Panels show (a) June 2021 mean temperature, (b) total precipitation and (c) standardised soil PC1 against elevation, and (d) soil-variable PC1 loadings. Points in a-c represent plots; lines show separate ordinary linear regressions and shading shows 95% confidence intervals. C, N, P and EC denote organic carbon, total nitrogen, extractable phosphorus and electrical conductivity. Equation x is elevation in metres.
