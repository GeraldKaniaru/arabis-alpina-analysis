Table 3. Baseline and projected reproductive trait summaries and changes under mid-century scenarios. Baseline values are conditional model predictions for June 2021; future values refer to 2041-2060 and are averaged equally across three climate models. For MISM, exponentiating log-scale predictions without a retransformation correction yields plant-level conditional medians; reported summaries average those medians, rather than estimating arithmetic expected seed mass. Other traits summarise expected values conditional on their predictors. Gaussian projections retain fitted block and plot effects. For seed number, two negligible random-intercept components were removed after the nested model failed numerical checks; the resolved model retains the NB2 family, log link and all environmental predictors. Change is future minus baseline. Flowering onset and duration are in days, MISM in mg per seed, and seed number in seeds per plant. Flowering duration is retained as the projection response; the H3 final-date re-expression does not change these projections. Confidence limits are 95% percentile bootstrap intervals for scenario mean changes, based on 1,000 retained replicates per trait with no refitting errors or non-finite predictions. Retention does not establish that all refits passed convergence-warning, Hessian or singularity checks. Equal model weighting does not quantify the full uncertainty among future climate trajectories. Reported values were rounded independently.
