Figure S7. Projected changes in Arabis alpina reproductive traits for 2041-2060 relative to June 2021. Panels show (a) flowering onset, (b) duration, (c) MISM and (d) seed number. Brown and yellow identify SSP2-4.5 and SSP5-8.5. Violin widths show densities of scenario mean changes from 1,000 parametric bootstrap replicates. Points show model-estimated changes averaged equally across three climate models. Bars are 95% percentile bootstrap intervals; dashed lines mark zero.
