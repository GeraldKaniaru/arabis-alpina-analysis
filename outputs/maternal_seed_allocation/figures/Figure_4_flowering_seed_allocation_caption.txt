Figure 4. Elevational associations and trait relationships in Arabis alpina. Panels: (a) elevation and flowering onset; (b) elevation and duration; (c) onset and final flowering date; (d) within-plot and among-plot onset coefficients for final flowering date; (e) elevation and natural-log MISM; (f) elevation and seed number; (g) MISM and seed number; (h) within-plot and among-plot mass-number coefficients. In c and d, coefficients describe final flowering date. Tests against zero describe completion timing; comparison with one addresses duration. The P value in c tests against zero; the primary H3 test against one gives P = 0.059. Lines and confidence bands show fixed-effect predictions; points in regression panels represent plants. Bars in d and h show 95% confidence intervals; dashed vertical lines mark zero. MISM is in mg per seed; f-h use a log link. Flowering day 1 is 1 June 2021. R2m and R2c denote marginal and conditional R-squared.
