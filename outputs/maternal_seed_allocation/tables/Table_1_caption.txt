Table 1. Mixed-effects estimates for H1-H6 and within-plot and among-plot associations (581 maternal plants). Elevation effects are per 100 m. All H3 estimates, intervals and R-squared values describe final flowering date. Subtract one from H3 coefficients and confidence limits for the corresponding duration association. For H3, P_zero tests a coefficient against zero and P_duration tests it against one; the P column tests zero for all rows. The primary H3 final-date slope is 0.947 (95% CI 0.892-1.002); P_duration = 0.059. H4 uses natural-log MISM; H5-H6 use a log link. MISM is mean individual seed mass (mg per seed). Marginal_R2 and Conditional_R2 denote marginal and conditional R-squared.
