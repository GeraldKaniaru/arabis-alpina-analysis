Table S1. Candidate-model comparisons for H1-H6 and seed-mass-germination models. Each comparison uses the same 581 maternal plants and random-effects structure. H1, H2, H4 and H5 each compare elevation alone, elevation + climate, elevation + soil, and elevation + climate + soil. Climate includes temperature and precipitation jointly; soil is soil PC1. H3 compares onset alone, onset + elevation, onset + elevation + climate, onset + elevation + soil, and onset + elevation + climate + soil. H3 AICc values were calculated from duration models and are equivalent for final flowering date with the same observations, onset predictor, covariates, random effects, maximum-likelihood fitting and residual covariance model. H3 coefficient tables and Figure 4 use final flowering date. H6 has one MISM candidate, not an environmental covariate comparison. H8 and germination time spread compare unadjusted and elevation-adjusted 100-seed-mass models within response. H7 is omitted. K is the parameter count; AICc is small-sample-corrected AIC; Delta_AICc and Akaike_weight quantify relative support within a candidate set.
